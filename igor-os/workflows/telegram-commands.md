# Igor OS Telegram Commands

Hermes exposes skills as slash commands. These commands are provided by the `igor-os/skills/*/SKILL.md` files.

```text
/morning
/evening
/work
/research
/dutch
/fishing
/training
/relationship
/weekly
/decision
/avoidance
/briefing_pack
/buying_decision
/field_note
/after_action
/energy
/dutch_real_life
/help
```

Examples:

```text
/morning priorities: QBR deck, partner follow-up, 20 min Dutch
/evening
/work turn these partner call notes into an escalation memo: ...
/research source plan for Amsterdam zander fishing in winter
/dutch give me a 10-minute drill for neighbourhood/admin Dutch
/fishing location: Nieuwe Meer, wind W 4 bft, perch, dropshot...
/training BJJ, low energy, worked half guard underhook, failed knee shield retention
/relationship suggest a small act of service tonight
/weekly
/decision should I switch Igor OS from Gemini Flash to Grok fast?
/avoidance I keep avoiding the partner follow-up
/briefing_pack tomorrow I have a QBR prep call with a partner
/buying_decision should I buy this spinning rod or wait?
/field_note heard "komt goed" at the gym today
/after_action partner call went well but I missed the commercial consequence
/energy slept 5h, knee sore, four meetings, need to write follow-up
/dutch_real_life making a dentist appointment
```
