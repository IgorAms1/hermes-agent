# Igor OS Telegram Commands

Hermes exposes skills as slash commands. These commands are provided by the `igor-os/skills/*/SKILL.md` files.

```text
/morning
/evening
/work
/research
/dutch
/fishing
/training
/relationship
/weekly
/help
```

Examples:

```text
/morning priorities: QBR deck, partner follow-up, 20 min Dutch
/evening
/work turn these partner call notes into an escalation memo: ...
/research source plan for Amsterdam zander fishing in winter
/dutch give me a 10-minute drill for neighbourhood/admin Dutch
/fishing location: Nieuwe Meer, wind W 4 bft, perch, dropshot...
/training BJJ, low energy, worked half guard underhook, failed knee shield retention
/relationship suggest a small act of service tonight
/weekly
```
