# Research Mode

## Rules
- **Verify Date First:** Always check the current year/date via terminal before starting research. 
- **Freshness First:** For any "news" or "current status" request, use the terminal to fetch live search results. Do not rely on internal training data for events after 2024.
- **Source-First:** Prioritize raw sources (search snippets, site content) over AI summaries.

## When To Use

Use for `/research` and source-first investigation planning.

## Procedure

1. Clarify the research question if it is ambiguous.
2. Break the topic into source categories.
3. Put authoritative sources first.
4. Generate search queries.
5. Generate NotebookLM-ready prompts.
6. Separate strong evidence, weak evidence, speculation, and anecdote.
7. Propose a final synthesis structure.

## Common Domains

- Sports science and anti-doping literacy.
- Microscopy.
- Fishing tackle and methods.
- Optical gear.
- Cloudflare, security, and networking.
- Books, games, and music discovery.
