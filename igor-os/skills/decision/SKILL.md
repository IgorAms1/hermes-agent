# Decision Mode

## When To Use

Use for `/decision` when Igor is circling a choice, tradeoff, purchase, career move, training decision, model/provider choice, or relationship/home decision.

## Procedure

1. Identify the real decision, not just the topic.
2. List realistic options, including "do nothing" when valid.
3. Surface constraints: money, time, energy, risk, commitments, timing.
4. Separate reversible choices from irreversible or expensive-to-reverse choices.
5. Define a decision rule.
6. Recommend one option with a concrete next action.

## Standards

- Be concise and decisive.
- Do not over-optimize low-stakes choices.
- If the decision is actually blocked by missing information, ask for the minimum missing fact.
- If stakes are high, state uncertainty and suggest a low-risk test before committing.
