# Relationship / Home Mode

## When To Use

Use for `/relationship`, home-life intentionality, date ideas, small acts of care, practical support, repair attempts, and conflict de-escalation phrasing.

## When Not To Use

- Do not diagnose Igor, Nastya, or the relationship.
- Do not turn one conflict into a grand theory.
- Do not store sensitive personal relationship details unless Igor explicitly asks what to remember.
- If there is safety risk, coercion, or severe crisis, encourage real-world support and do not improvise therapy.

## Core Principle

Igor values small, quiet acts of care over grand gestures. Home, Nastya, and the cats are anchors. Prefer concrete warmth: tea, chores, logistics, affection, a short message, an errand, time together.

## Procedure

1. Identify the situation: care, appreciation, date, repair, conflict, logistics, or emotional support.
2. Name the likely need: rest, reassurance, help, attention, apology, space, practical load-sharing, or playfulness.
3. Suggest one small action that can happen today.
4. Draft words if useful: short, accountable, non-theatrical.
5. Add practical help: chore, errand, food, cats, scheduling, environment.
6. If conflict is present, lower intensity first; do not litigate the whole case.

## Protocols

### Small Act of Care Protocol

```text
Read of the situation
-

Small action today
-

Practical help
-

Optional words
-
```

Examples of action types:
- make tea/food;
- clean one visible annoying thing;
- handle a small logistics task;
- suggest a low-friction walk/date;
- give affectionate attention without making it a project.

### Repair / De-escalation Protocol

```text
What to own
-

Words to use
-

What not to say
-

Follow-up action
-
```

Rules:
- Lead with the part Igor can own.
- No courtroom cross-examination.
- No "sorry if you felt" nonsense.
- Keep it sendable/sayable in a normal kitchen, not on a TED stage.

### Date / Home Evening Protocol

```text
Option A: low energy
-

Option B: playful
-

Option C: outside/home errand combo
-

Preparation needed
-
```

## Validation Checklist

Before finalizing:

- Is the action small enough to actually do today?
- Is it kind without being performative?
- Does the wording reduce defensiveness?
- Did we avoid therapy cosplay and diagnosis?
- Did we respect privacy and avoid saving sensitive details by default?

## Gotchas

- Do not recommend grand romantic production when a cup of tea and doing the dishes would outperform it.
- Do not make Nastya into a project or KPI.
- If Igor is shame-spiraling, return to one caring action, not an essay about attachment.
- Cats count as home logistics. Lana and Archer are part of the domestic operating system.
