# Compensation Proposal Pattern (Semaphore, May 2026)

## Context

Katya proactively asked Igor «что ты хочешь за это?» (27 May 2026). Igor didn't name a number. The team is moving toward formalizing his role — he's been accidentally added to the founders' chat, NDA discussions are happening.

## Two-Track Model (developed 28 May 2026)

### Track 1: Ongoing GTM / Advisory
**What it covers:** pricing, packaging, sales process, routing (simple/mid-market/enterprise), positioning, outbound, partner motion, playbooks, coaching Кати/Никиты/Вики.

**Model:** Retainer €2-3K/month (flexible). Deferred start to August if cash flow tight.

### Track 2: Deal Involvement / Revenue Support
**What it covers:** participation in specific opportunities — discovery, qualification, customer/partner calls, pricing strategy, proposal, negotiation/procurement, close plan, partner enablement.

**Material involvement threshold:** minimum 2 significant elements from the list above.

**Model:**
- Direct deals: 5% first-year ARR (fallback 3-5%)
- Partner/channel: 5% net ARR for 24 months — applies to partners Igor introduced, activated, enabled, or materially supported
- Strategic kicker (€50K+ ARR): keep in pocket, not for first conversation

### Key Formulation
> «Я хочу разделить ongoing GTM/advisory работу и involvement в конкретные opportunities. Advisory можно оформить через retainer/fractional scope, а если я materially involved в сделку — discovery, customer calls, pricing, negotiation, partner enablement, close strategy — тогда нужен отдельный success component.»

### Positioning
> «У меня нет срочности по оплате. Я здесь не из-за денег прямо сейчас — я вижу, куда это идёт, и хочу, чтобы конструкция была правильной с самого начала.»

### Red Line
Deal component is non-negotiable. Without it, Igor closes revenue-generating deals for free under the guise of consulting/advisory.
