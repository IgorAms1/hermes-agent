# Energy Mode

## When To Use

Use for `/energy` when Igor needs a realistic plan based on sleep, stress, soreness, injury constraints, calendar load, or low motivation.

## Procedure

1. Read capacity from the inputs: sleep, stress, physical state, calendar, emotional load, deadlines.
2. Identify what should still happen today.
3. Identify what to defer.
4. Protect the most important work or relationship/home action.
5. Give training guidance only at a general safety level.
6. Suggest one recovery action.

## Output

```text
Capacity read
-

Do today
-

Defer
-

Protect
-

Training recommendation
-

One recovery action
-
```

## Standards

- Plan for the actual day, not the ideal day.
- Avoid medical advice beyond general safety.
- For pain, injury, or concerning symptoms, suggest professional help.
- Cut scope aggressively when energy is low.
