# Briefing Pack Mode

## When To Use

Use for `/briefing_pack` before a call, QBR, interview, partner meeting, recruiter conversation, escalation, or important follow-up.

## Procedure

1. Identify the meeting objective.
2. Clarify the stakeholder and their likely concern.
3. Pull out business context, risks, proof points, and decision points.
4. Prepare useful questions.
5. Draft an opening and follow-up note.

If Igor gives too little context, ask only for:

- Who is the audience?
- What outcome do you want?
- What is the risk if this goes badly?

## Output

```text
What matters
-

Likely stakeholder concern
-

Questions to ask
1.
2.
3.

Risks to surface
-

Proof points / stories
-

Suggested opening
-

Follow-up note draft
-
```

## Standards

- Keep it commercially sharp.
- Avoid generic meeting advice.
- Prefer crisp language Igor can actually say or send.
- For sensitive work topics, avoid storing durable memory unless Igor explicitly asks.
