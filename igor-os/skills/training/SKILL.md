# MTB / BJJ / Training Mode

## When To Use

Use for `/training`, MTB, BJJ, strength, conditioning, recovery, mobility, pain-aware programming, and technique logs.

## When Not To Use

- Acute injury, neurological symptoms, severe pain, or medical concern → advise professional medical assessment.
- Nutrition/medical treatment plans beyond general safety → keep conservative.
- Work/life planning unless directly affecting training load or recovery.

## Non-Negotiable Safety Context

Igor's lower back currently has veto power.

Avoid or strongly downshift:

- kettlebell cleans, swings, snatches, ABCs, heavy ballistic hinge work;
- 2×24kg ABC / clean & press style sessions when back is irritated;
- stacking hard hinge work with long hikes, sand walking, MTB fatigue, or poor sleep.

Prefer:

- mobility, hangs/pull-ups, deep KB push-ups, controlled split squats, dead bug, bird dog, side plank, glute bridge;
- careful Bulgarian split squats with bodyweight or 16kg;
- hard days only 1-2×/week while back is sensitive;
- chest progression via deep KB push-ups.

## Procedure

1. Capture session type, duration, energy, sleep/recovery if mentioned.
2. Capture pain status: before / during / after, especially back.
3. Identify training goal: strength, MTB skill, BJJ technique, conditioning, recovery, or rehab-adjacent maintenance.
4. Extract what worked and what failed.
5. Name the pattern/risk: load spike, hinge sensitivity, fatigue stacking, technique issue, confidence gap, or recovery debt.
6. Give one next progression cue.
7. Keep progression conservative: change only one variable at a time.

## Capture Fields

- Session type.
- Energy level.
- Pain/injury constraints.
- Exercises / rounds / route / technique focus.
- What worked.
- What failed.
- Recovery signal after session.
- Next progression cue.


Rules:
- No ballistic hinge if back is achy, uncertain, or recently irritated.
- Use RPE 6-7 unless Igor explicitly reports feeling robust and pain-free.
- Stop if pain sharpens, radiates, changes gait, or persists/worsens after warm-up.

### MTB Skill Log Protocol

Track:
- trail/spot;
- feature: jump, manual, corner, braking, bowl riding, body position;
- fear/confidence level;
- cue that helped;
- one repeatable next drill.

### BJJ Log Protocol

Track:
- position/theme;
- recurring mistake;
- successful cue;
- sparring intensity;
- one technical question for next class.

## Validation Checklist

Before finalizing:

- Did we account for back status?
- Did we avoid ballistic hinge recommendations when risk is present?
- Is the next cue concrete and small?
- Did we change only one training variable?
- Did we avoid pretending to be a doctor?

## Gotchas

- Do not frame backing off as weakness. The body is voting; count the votes.
- Do not recommend "just stretch more" as magic dust.
- Do not stack hard lower-body work, MTB intensity, and long hikes without recovery logic.
- If Igor reports feeling great, still avoid sudden heroic programming. Heroism is how backs file complaints.
