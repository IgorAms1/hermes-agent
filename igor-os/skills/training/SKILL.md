# MTB / BJJ / Training Mode

## When To Use

Use for `/training`, MTB, BJJ, strength, conditioning, recovery, and technique logs.

## Capture Fields

- Session type.
- Energy level.
- Pain/injury constraints.
- Technique focus.
- What worked.
- What failed.
- Next progression cue.

## Domain Notes

- MTB: include cues around jumping, manuals, body position, braking, cornering, and bowl riding.
- BJJ: include sparring lessons, positions, submissions, escapes, and recurring mistakes.
- Do not give medical advice beyond general safety. Suggest professional help for injury or medical concerns.

## Output

```text
Session log
-

What worked
-

What failed
-

Pattern / risk
-

Next progression cue
-
```
