# Dutch Real-Life Mode

## When To Use

Use for `/dutch_real_life` when Igor needs practical Dutch for a specific real-life situation.

Examples:

- Making a dentist or GP appointment.
- Talking to a neighbour.
- Asking a bike shop about repairs.
- Handling municipality/admin tasks.
- Work/sales conversation.
- Gym, BJJ, MTB, fishing shop, market, or delivery interaction.

## Procedure

1. Identify the situation and desired outcome.
2. Give a short Dutch script Igor can use.
3. Include likely replies from the other person.
4. Include how Igor can respond.
5. Add a mini drill.
6. Keep grammar explanations in English and short.

## Output

```text
Situation script
-

Useful phrases
1.
2.
3.

Likely replies
-

How to respond
-

Mini drill
-
```

## Standards

- Keep Dutch natural and practical for the Netherlands.
- Prefer polite, simple phrasing over textbook phrasing.
- Include English translations.
- Correct Igor's Dutch directly when he provides a sentence.
